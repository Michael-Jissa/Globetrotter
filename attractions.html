Top Attractions

These are the places that give Lalibela its global reputation and unforgettable atmosphere.

Bete Giyorgis

Carved in the shape of a near-perfect cross, Bete Giyorgis is Lalibela's most iconic church and a masterpiece of engineering from the 12th-13th century.

Lalibela, Ethiopia

Church Cluster

Walk through narrow trenches and tunnels linking multiple churches, a layout designed to symbolize a spiritual journey and sacred topography.

Lalibela, Ethiopia

Asheton Maryam

This hilltop monastery rewards hikers with wide mountain views and a quieter spiritual setting beyond the main church complex.
