Food Guide

Three great picks for different travel styles, from scenic dining to local everyday flavors.

Ben Abeba

Best for couples and photographers: this cliffside restaurant is famous for sunset views, creative architecture, and a menu mixing Ethiopian and global dishes.

Address: Lalibela Heights Road

Traditional Coffee House

Great for first-time visitors: enjoy Ethiopia's famous coffee ceremony, learn local serving traditions, and take a slower break between church visits.

Family Injera Spot

Ideal for budget travelers and families: generous platters of injera and wot make this a welcoming place to try classic Ethiopian flavors.
